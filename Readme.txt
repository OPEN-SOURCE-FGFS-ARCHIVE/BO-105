INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:
-----------------------------

1.- Copy the folder "bo-105" on "data/Aircraft" folder.
2.- Replace existing files.

NOTE 1: Make a backup of the original folder, in case having troubles with the new installed files.
NOTE 2: This is an unfinished work, and it's still in development.
NOTE 3: This update has been developed to run better under FlightGear v. 2.0.0
___________________________________________

CHANGES ON THIS UPDATE:
-----------------------------------------

* Entire panel developed in 3D.
* Entire re texture of panel, and some parts of the cockpit and chassis.
* All instruments are now included on the "bo-105/Instruments" folder, for its better re texture and modifications to adapt them to the new panel.
* Most instruments position have been modificated to adapt them to the new model.
* All interior glasses duplicated, to add "glass effect" (reflections of real image).
* Pilot and co-pilot have been deleted to free space to the new model and textures.
* Cockpit and Chassis are actually in separated models to better manipulate them, until every changes are done, when I will join them in a single model.
* 2 new views: co-pilot and passenger.
* A little modification on the NAS file, to get a smooth and less sharp movement with "dynamic cockpit" option.
* Two simple liveries has been added: one black with the FGFS logo, and other with greay camouflage and the chilean national emblem, this until it's finished a real livery.
